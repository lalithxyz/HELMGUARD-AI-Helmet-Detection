# HELMGUARD – Results

## Detection Results

The trained model is designed to identify two helmet conditions:

| Condition | Detection Result | Ignition |
|---|---|---|
| Rider wearing helmet | Helmet Detected | ON |
| Rider without helmet | No Helmet Found | OFF |

## Expected System Behavior

### Helmet

Input: Rider wearing a helmet

Output:

Helmet Detected

Ignition ON

### No Helmet

Input: Rider without a helmet

Output:

No Helmet Found

Ignition OFF

## Outcome

The project demonstrates the integration of artificial intelligence, computer vision and embedded-system control for improving two-wheeler rider safety.
